# AI Data Pipeline

## Objective

Design the flow of AI data across the Zecpath platform.

## Data Flow

Resume Upload
↓
Resume Extraction
↓
Resume Parsing
↓
Parsed Candidate Profile
↓
ATS Scoring
↓
Candidate Screening
↓
AI Interview
↓
Interview Results
↓
Hiring Decision

## Pipeline Stages

1. Resume Upload
   - Candidate uploads resume.

2. Resume Extraction
   - Extract text from PDF or DOCX files.

3. Resume Parsing
   - Extract skills, experience, and education.

4. Parsed Candidate Profile
   - Store structured candidate information.

5. ATS Scoring
   - Compare candidate information with job requirements.

6. Candidate Screening
   - Evaluate candidate eligibility.

7. AI Interview
   - Process interview responses.

8. Interview Results
   - Store interview scores and feedback.

9. Hiring Decision
   - Use candidate scores and interview results to support the hiring decision.
