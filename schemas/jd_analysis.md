## 5. Experience Patterns

Job descriptions commonly specify:

- Minimum years of experience
- Relevant domain experience
- Required technical experience
- Experience with specific tools or technologies
- Responsibilities associated with the role

The sample JDs contain experience requirements ranging from approximately 1 to 5 years.

## 6. Common Job Description Structure

A typical job description contains:

1. Job Title
2. Domain / Department
3. Location
4. Employment Type
5. Job Responsibilities
6. Required Skills
7. Preferred Skills
8. Experience Requirements
9. Education Requirements
10. Certifications

## 7. Key Observation

Job descriptions from different domains have different skills and qualifications, but they follow a similar overall structure.

This structured information can be used by Zecpath AI for job matching, candidate eligibility checking, ATS scoring and candidate ranking.
