## 3. Experience Patterns

The resumes commonly contain:

- Job designation
- Company name
- Years of experience
- Job responsibilities
- Technologies or tools used
- Career progression

Experience ranges in the sample resumes from approximately 2 to 5 years.

## 6. Resume Structure Identified

A common structured resume can contain:

1. Candidate Information
2. Professional Summary
3. Education
4. Work Experience
5. Skills
6. Certifications

## 7. Key Observation

Although resumes belong to different domains, many of them contain common information such as candidate details, education, experience, skills and certifications.

This information can be converted from unstructured resume text into structured data for Zecpath AI's resume parsing and candidate matching systems.
